## There are TWO methods inside Beta Rebellion. Don't confuse them.

### PART 1 — The $10k Beta Method (THIS WEBINAR IS ABOUT THIS)
How to generate your first ~$10k **before the course is built**, with **no list, no ads, no sales skills.** The buyers come from her **personal network**, via **direct invitations** (not posting into the ether and hoping).

The flow:
1. **List people you already know** who have the problem your course solves: coworkers, family, friends, people from courses you've taken, church, community.
2. **Invite them to a ~15-min "research call"** (directly, or via FB/IG/email if she has one). The frame is research, NOT a pitch. Example (weight-loss creator to her husband's sister who's trying to lose weight): *"I'm thinking about putting together a program on how I lost 100 pounds. I know you're on a weight loss journey. Would you be willing to be interviewed for research to help me make the best program I can?"* → easy yes.
3. **Run the research call.** Genuinely understand the ideal client's problem at a deeper level. This is NOT for show, she desperately needs it. It builds the actual program, and sometimes reveals she doesn't even want to do this course.
4. **Book the second call AT THE END of the first call.** Critical. Preemptively set the time to present the finished idea, get feedback, and see if they'd want to be a beta tester. Booking on the spot prevents losing them to lost interest. Frame: *"When I've done my interviews and put the program together, would you be open to maybe being a beta tester? It's not free, but it's a vastly reduced cost. Let's get on a second call, I'll show you my plan, get your feedback, and see if it's even something you'd want."*
5. **The second call = enrollment.** Warm and easy because they've been softly introduced, they know a beta is coming, they know they're reviewing a pathway. Enroll them at a **vastly reduced beta price, minimum ~$500.**
6. **The math:** ~20 people × $500 = **$10,000.** That $10k is seed money: grow the email list, get first clients, or fund a real ad launch.
7. **Repeatable.** People run this again and again: beta → refine → beta → or pivot → beta. An income generator that needs no ads and no list. Results: usually ~$10k, as high as $50k.
- **What replaces "sales skills":** the research call does the rapport and the soft pre-sell. The structure sells, not slick sales ability.

### PART 2 — The Live Beta Filming Method (the LAUNCH mechanism, after the offer is validated)
Once she has a validated offer and wants a bigger launch:
- Take **ONE module of your signature course** and teach it live (free version = "live beta").
- **Get people in via ads:** *"I'm filming a course on XYZ. Want to watch me film it? Come in free and get the course free in exchange for feedback and testimonials."* People enter intending to be beta testers.
- **Structure:** Week 1 = "come watch me film the course" (teach, deliver a real win). Week 2 = the selling week (invite them to be sold to, sell at the end).
- **Jenna's own proof:** her live beta filming method generated **$6M for Secret AI Society** by teaching brand identity (logo, colors, mood board) for free, then: *"Want to execute your brand, do images, build a website? Come take Secret AI Society."*

## THE SHIFT (current evolution — context, not yet in the program)
- **Free launches aren't converting like they used to. Low-ticket → paid is what's working now.**
- New model: **paid challenges / 5-day bootcamps.** Get people in via ads on a low-ticket offer (~$99–$199) into a 5-day bootcamp, teach something that gets a real WIN, then sell at the end. Requires ad money. Mechanically very similar to live beta filming (teach a win → upsell).
- **Current state of the program:** Beta Rebellion currently teaches the live beta filming method (free). As Jenna runs her own paid bootcamp launch and cracks it, she'll ADD the paid bootcamp method as the new way to launch into the program.

## Where this leaves the webinar
The webinar promise ("$10k before your course is built, no list, no sales skills") = **PART 1, the $10k Beta Method.** The Live Beta Filming Method and the paid-bootcamp evolution are the launch/scale mechanisms that come AFTER, funded by that first $10k.
