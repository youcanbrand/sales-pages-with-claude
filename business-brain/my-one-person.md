# my-one-person.md — Lisa

_The ONE real person we serve. Write everything to her. "Busy women" is a category, not a person. Lisa is a person._

## Who she is
**Lisa** — an astrologer and business coach who realized her real passion is astrology, not generic business coaching. She's spent years developing her own astrology methodology and wants to help people reach their goals with it. She only got that clarity **after going through Beta Rebellion** — which is why she's both our avatar AND living proof the program works.

She's a coach / consultant / course creator who **has not yet generated her first $100k** from a course, membership, or group program. She's a serial launcher — hops from thing to thing chasing something that sticks, feels right, *and* makes money.

## The moment + trigger (the day she finds Jenna)
She's overwhelmed. She wants to launch her thing — and an app — and she genuinely doesn't know where to start or where to get the best information. She's already bought other programs, but they were so overwhelming she bailed. She's looking for a **simple process** she can actually follow.

## What she's FEELING (not what she needs)
- **Shame** about pivoting again. Shame about "not being launched yet." 
- **Shame in her relationship** — her family keeps watching her fail.
- **Overwhelm** — too many moving parts, no clear first step.

## Her exact words (use these — they're subject lines and webinar hooks)
- "If I can't launch this and make actual revenue, I'm going back to my day job."
- "I'm overwhelmed with everything I have to do — I want to launch this AND an app and I don't know where to start."
- "I've invested in other programs but they were so overwhelming — I just want a simple process."

## The ONE transformation Jenna is certain she can give her
**Certainty.** Help Lisa figure out if this is THE thing to build her whole business around — **or** free her by proving it's not. Then hand her a simple, repeatable way to research what people will actually pay for, and build offers that get clients results AND feel good to deliver. **Proof in her body first, proof in the bank second.**

> This is NOT a "make six figures" promise. It's a "stop guessing and finally KNOW you're on the right path — with real proof — before you waste another year" promise. That honesty is the differentiator.

## Her life AFTER (granular)
It's Tuesday morning. She's not refreshing Stripe with her stomach in knots — she already knows this offer works because she validated it before she built it. She opens her laptop to messages from two people who just paid, and it doesn't feel like a fluke: she knows exactly why they bought and how to get the next one. She thinks **"Finally, something is working…"** — and she's motivated, clear, and confident this is the road to go down. For the first time she tells her partner "this is working," and actually believes it.
